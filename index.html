My story

Most people pick a lane — frontend, backend, ML, cloud. I never did. I'm a CS student at VIT-AP specialising in Data Analytics (CGPA 9.07), currently interning at Visteon Corporation where I get to see what production-grade automotive software actually looks like under the hood.

My projects don't start as projects. They start as problems I can't stop thinking about. At Sabudh Foundation, that problem was India's EV infrastructure gap — I built a full ML pipeline to forecast charging demand and used genetic algorithms to figure out where stations would actually matter, not just where they'd fit. At a hackathon, it was the question of whether a city street is safe at 11pm for a woman walking alone — Citadel Maps answered it with GIS and community data, built overnight at CodeStorm 2025. SwarSmriti asked what happens to someone's voice and memories after they're gone — built at Google CodeClash, it made people cry in the demo. Project Eidolon asked how attackers behave when they think no one is watching — the answer was a 50-node honeypot network that lets me find out. MERN, FastAPI, Docker Swarm, AWS, LLMs — whatever the problem needs.

The best engineers I admire don't talk about what they'll build. They show you what already runs.
Think
Problems first, tools second. I like to understand the why deeply before deciding the how.
Build
Full-stack + ML engineer. MERN, FastAPI, Docker Swarm, LLMs — whatever the problem calls for.
Ship
From localhost to production —
I like seeing things land in the real world, not just in a readme.
